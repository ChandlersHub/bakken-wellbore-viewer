    Data sources and limitations

    • Survey, operator, production and spacing-unit records come from North Dakota DMR public downloads. Each view states its snapshot date.
    • This is research tooling. It is not a reserve estimate, title opinion or economic valuation.
    • DSU depth boxes and lateral-footage scenarios are approximate, editable display models, not legal boundaries or drillable-location counts.
    • Missing production stays missing; it is never shown as zero. Incomplete survey trajectories are not filled in.
